## Limitations and Archival Status

Study A and Study B partial results are excluded from the replacement aggregate.
Evidence is isolated OpenPLC laboratory measurement using read-only Modbus/TCP
FC3, not synthetic-test performance or production validation. Conclusions apply
to the documented timing setup and five impairments; no universal superiority,
DNP3 validation, EtherNet/IP validation, full GRFICS validation, external replication,
or industry adoption is claimed. Resource instrumentation covers prediction only.

A persistent identifier for v0.6 will be added after archival. No DOI is assigned
here or in current citation metadata. The v0.5.1-alpha DOI retained as historical
context in README identifies only that previous archived release.
