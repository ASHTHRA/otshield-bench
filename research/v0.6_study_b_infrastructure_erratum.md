## Preservation and infrastructure correction

Study B remains preserved without modification. It will not be mixed into the
final replacement study and must be disclosed as a failed attempt, alongside
Study A (`20260920T203716Z`; see `v0.6_duplicate_tid_erratum.md`).

The runner now takes a nonblocking exclusive host lock before creating study
output or launching the worker and holds it through worker termination and
final accounting. The worker verifies inherited lock ownership before cleanup
can run. OpenPLC's full container ID is captured once per trial; identity and
running state are checked at critical condition lifecycle points. A missing,
stopped, or replaced OpenPLC fails closed without restarting, recreating,
retrying, or proceeding to normalization/evaluation after the failed check.

This infrastructure hardening does **not** change the preregistered scientific
design: one OpenPLC instance per trial, five rotating conditions, 25 trials,
60 FC3 transactions per condition, client-egress impairment, server-namespace
capture, frozen thresholds and historical calibration, canonicalization,
paired evaluation, aggregation, and Student-t intervals remain unchanged.
There is no automatic study retry. Infrastructure tests use synthetic/mocked
responses and do not constitute laboratory evidence. No replacement Study C
was launched as part of this hardening.
