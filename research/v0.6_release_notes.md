## Major Changes from v0.5.1-alpha

- A completed 25-trial, five-condition paired OpenPLC study replaces the historical
  five-trial scale for this release: 125 completed condition runs, 60 planned
  transactions/run, 7,500 planned transactions, and 250 detector evaluations.
- `robust-polling-burst-v1` evaluates the same captured traffic as the unchanged
  50 ms `polling-burst-v1` baseline. Its 100.23721899414062 ms threshold is frozen
  from 195 eligible normal intervals in the historical v0.5 clean evidence.
  No v0.6 test data tunes or replaces calibration.
- Paired recall/F1 effects and two-sided 95% Student-t run-level intervals accompany
  effectiveness, coverage, confusion counts, and measured prediction resource costs.
- A separate readiness gate checks host prerequisites and, with explicit startup
  authorization, bounded read-only FC3 service readiness. It creates no study results.
- Advisory runner locking, inherited worker ownership, and OpenPLC identity checks
  harden execution against concurrent lifecycle interference and fail closed.
- A narrowly defined, audited v0.6 transport canonicalization handles an earlier
  foreign response-only duplicate without modifying raw normalization or generic
  ingestion behavior. Existing benchmark abstractions and v0.5 interfaces remain.

## Completed Study and Findings

The source of truth is
[aggregate.json](../evidence/v06/20260922T021855Z/aggregate.json), with completion in
[study.json](../evidence/v06/20260922T021855Z/study.json). Both detectors have mean
recall/F1 1.0/1.0 for `clean` and `delay20`, and 0.95/0.9737564284932706 for
`delay20_jitter5_loss5`. At `delay40_jitter10`, baseline recall/F1 is
0.46/0.6230102454707571 versus robust 1.0/1.0; paired recall improves by 0.54
(95% CI 0.4962180999417499–0.5837819000582501). At `delay60_jitter10`, baseline
recall/F1 is 0.0/0.0 versus robust 1.0/1.0. Coverage remains 1.0 and false positives
remain zero across all conditions.

Within the documented isolated OpenPLC lab and preregistered timing setup, the
independently calibrated robust threshold showed materially greater resilience
under the heavier tested delay conditions, with no observed recall/F1 penalty
under clean and lighter tested conditions.

## Reproducibility and Limitations

The [release manifest](v0.6_release_manifest.md) links execution provenance,
versioned schemas, frozen calibration, and integrity records. The
[technical report](OTShield_Bench_v0.6_Technical_Report.md) and
[findings](v0.6_findings.md) document results and interpretation. Local software
verification uses `./scripts/verify_release.sh` (whitespace, tests, source/wheel
builds); it launches no Docker/OpenPLC experiment.

Evidence covers only the documented isolated OpenPLC lab and read-only Modbus/TCP
FC3 timing workload. Synthetic tests are not measured laboratory evidence.
Prediction CPU/wall time and traced Python allocations do not measure full
deployment cost. There is no production validation, universal superiority,
DNP3 or EtherNet/IP validation, full GRFICS validation, external replication, or
industry adoption claim. DNP3 and EtherNet/IP remain deferred until Modbus is stable.
